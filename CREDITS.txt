3D models (Creative Commons Attribution 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The models were resized, recoloured and optimized for this game. In-game car names are our own.
The full list with authors and source links is on the in-game Credits screen (src/assets.js, CREDITS).

Skies: Poly Haven (CC0). Surface textures: ambientCG (CC0).
Engine: three.js (MIT). Physics: Rapier (Apache 2.0).
Sound and music are generated in the browser by the game code.
